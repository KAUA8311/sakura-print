## 📱 Connect Your Phone

Now comes the fun part – connecting your phone. sakura-print uses a technology called AirPrint, which is built into most modern phones.

### For iPhone or iPad (Apple devices)

1. Make sure your phone and your computer are connected to the **same Wi-Fi network**.
2. Open any app that has a share or print option (like Photos, Safari, or Mail).
3. Tap the **share icon** (a square with an arrow pointing up).
4. Scroll down and tap **"Print"**.
5. You should see sakura-print listed as a printer. Tap on it.
6. Choose the number of copies, select the page range if you want, and tap **"Print"**.

That's it! Your document will start printing within a few seconds.

### For Android Phones

1. Make sure your phone and computer are on the **same Wi-Fi network**.
2. Open the document or photo you want to print.
3. Tap the **three-dot menu** (⋮) in the corner.
4. Select **"Print"** from the menu.
5. Choose sakura-print from the list of printers.
6. Adjust the settings if needed and tap the **printer icon** to print.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

If something isn't working, try these simple fixes before reaching out for help:

### Problem: My phone can't find sakura-print
- Make sure your phone and computer are on the **same Wi-Fi network**.
- Restart the sakura-print app on your computer.
- Temporarily turn off your phone's Wi-Fi and turn it back on.

### Problem: The print is blank or garbled
- Check that the printer has paper and ink/toner.
- Turn the printer off and on again.
- Restart your computer.

### Problem: Printing is slow
- This is normal for large photos or documents. Try printing a smaller test page first.
- Move your phone closer to your Wi-Fi router.

### Problem: The printer shows "offline"
- Make sure the printer is turned on.
- Check the cable or Wi-Fi connection between the printer and your computer.
